# results/

- `figures/`  error pictures of single scenes, drawn by `notebooks/08_error_pictures.ipynb`.

The numbers are not copied here. Every table of the write-up is printed in the saved output of the notebook
that computed it (`04_report`, `07_test_report`, `09_moving_object_edges`), and `docs/decisions.md` records
each of them with its source. Predictions, ground truth and per-scene metrics live in the persistent storage
of whoever runs the notebooks (several GB); they are rebuilt by running them.

Licence of the pictures: they show FZI-AURA camera images and LiDAR data (CC BY-SA 4.0, FZI Forschungszentrum
Informatik) and are adaptations of them, so they are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 as well. The depth shown is
the output of VGGT-Omega (FAIR Noncommercial Research License): noncommercial research use only.
